🏆 Makuuchi Yūshō · Onosato (12-3)

大の里 Onosato
East Yokozuna · sixth Emperor's Cup, won the old-fashioned way: head to head on senshūraku. Onosato stood alone at 11-1 after Day 12, then lost to Kirishima on Day 13 and Kotozakura on Day 14, which let Ōzeki Aonishiki draw level at 11-3. The two of them met on senshūraku. Onosato won by tsukidashi, so no playoff was needed.

Final leaderboard: Onosato 12 · Aonishiki and Fujinokawa (S1W) 11 · Churanoumi, Asanoyama, Ura and Wakamotoharu 10. Sanshō: Kantō-shō to Fujinokawa and Churanoumi (the second for each); no Shukun-shō, no Ginō-shō. Kyūjō: Yokozuna Hoshoryu, Wakatakakage and Wakanosho from Day 1, Kotoshoho from Day 12, Takanosho from Day 13. Jūryō yūshō: Kitanowaka.
