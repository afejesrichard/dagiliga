🏆 Makuuchi Yūshō · Onosato (13-2, playoff)

大の里 Onosato
East Yokozuna · seventh Emperor's Cup, and the second in a row, won in a playoff. Onosato lost his opening bout to Komusubi Yoshinofuji, then lost only once more, to Sekiwake Atamifuji on Day 10. Maegashira 7 Wakamotoharu led alone at 11-1 until Onosato beat him on Day 13. Both won out to finish 13-2, Wakamotoharu knocking Atamifuji out of the race on senshūraku, and the yokozuna took the playoff by watashikomi.

Final leaderboard: Onosato and Wakamotoharu 13 · Kinbozan, Atamifuji and Asahakuryu 11 · Kirishima, Asanoyama, Roga, Takanosho and Tamawashi 10. Sanshō went to Wakamotoharu, Atamifuji, Kinbozan and 42-year-old Tamawashi. Yokozuna Hoshoryu finished 9-6 after giving up four kinboshi. Ōzeki Aonishiki went 7-8. Kyūjō: Kotoeiho (Days 3 to 9, returned and finished 7-2-6), Asakoryu and Shishi from Day 11. Shishi finished 0-11.
