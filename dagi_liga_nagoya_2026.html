🏆 Makuuchi Yusho — Aonishiki (12-3)

安青錦 Aonishiki
West Sekiwake · Ajigawa Stable · maiden top-division championship, taken out of a rare three-way yusho-kettei-sen against sekiwake Atamifuji and ozeki Kirishima — all three finished regulation at 12-3. The 22-year-old from Vinnytsia, Ukraine lost his final regulation bout to Atamifuji and then beat him in the playoff to take the Emperor's Cup anyway. He also collected the Ginosho (Technique Prize).

Sansho special prizes: Shukunsho to Fujinokawa (M1E), who took two kinboshi off the yokozuna · Kantosho shared by Atamifuji, Kotoeiho and Takayasu · Ginosho to Aonishiki. Yokozuna Hoshoryu went kyujo after Day 13.
